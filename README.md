# Analyzing automobile purchase behaviour to identify key customer drivers

## Project Overview
Businesses in the automobile industry often struggle to identify the key factors influencing customer purchase decisions. This project analyzes customer data to identify patterns and drivers that impact buying behavour. 

## Business Questions Answered
- Do men prefer SUVs more than women?
- What is the likelihood of salaried individuals buying sedans?
- Does partner income influence premium car purchases?
- How do loans impact automobile spending?

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Statistical Analysis

## Key Insights
- Customers in higher income groups show significantly higher purchase probability 
- Age and income together are strong predictors of buying behavoiur
- certain customer segments demonstrate higher engagement and conversion potential

## Business Recommendations 
- Target high-income customer segmenst with premium product positioning
- Perrsonalize marketing campaigns based on demographics segments
- Focus advertising efforts on high-conversion customer groups
- Optimize pricing strategies based on customer affordability patterns

## Business impact 
This analysis helps besinesses improve marketing effectiveness, target the right customer segments, and increase conversion rates through data-driven decision-making.
